    Accounts

    Connect your banks

    Bank connections use Plaid, which works with 12,000+ banks and card issuers. To turn it on:

    1. Create a free account at dashboard.plaid.com and copy your client ID and secret from Developers → Keys.
    2. Copy .env.example to .env and fill in PLAID_CLIENT_ID and PLAID_SECRET. Start with PLAID_ENV=sandbox to try it with fake banks, then switch to production for your real accounts.
    3. Restart the app.

    Until then you can import CSV statements below, or load demo data to explore.

    AccountInstitutionTypeBalance

    Import a CSV statement
